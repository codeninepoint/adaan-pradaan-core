About TenantPlatform

A platform where every
contributor is an owner

We are building a cloud marketplace where the people who create value — plugin authors, integration builders, and service architects — share in the rewards they generate, not just the employees of a corporation.

🎯
Our Mission

To build a multi-tenant cloud platform where any individual, team, or organization can publish technical services — plugins, integrations, and offerings — and earn a fair, transparent share of the revenue they generate, governed by code and not by corporate hierarchy.

🌐
Our Vision

A world where the platform is a commons — not a landlord. Every contributor who builds something that delivers value receives a proportional, automatic reward. The ecosystem grows because the people who grow it benefit directly.

"Contributors, not employees. Owners, not tenants."
Anyone who ships a plugin, integration, or service that earns revenue on this platform is a stakeholder — not a vendor subject to arbitrary platform fees, and not an employee subject to a payroll.

Confirm slug is unique within org and caller is org-admin or owner.
Step 2 — current
Create tenant record (status: pending)
INSERT tenant.tenants with status=pending. Idempotent — safe to retry.
Step 3
Seed default project
INSERT tenant.projects (name=“default”, slug=“default”).
Step 4
Seed RBAC roles (tenant-admin, resource-admin, viewer)
INSERT authz.roles x3 with is_system=true, scope_id=new_tenant_id.

Assign caller as tenant-admin

Activate tenant (status: active)
